title: Metrics
description: Every series the host's Prometheus endpoint publishes.
---

What the host serves at `/metrics`, from the `[metrics]` section of `config.toml`. Every series is
prefixed `nibrunner_`, and what follows names what is being measured. Anything labelled `app` is
about one app and nothing else, which is what a per-app dashboard filters on.

A `histogram` publishes three series the table does not list one by one: `_bucket` with an extra
`le` label, `_sum` and `_count`.
